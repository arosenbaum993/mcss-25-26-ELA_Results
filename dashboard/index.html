One strength, three gaps

TypeAreaResultWhat it means

Three moves for this year's Lit & Comp II classes

Last year's testers have finished the course, so these moves are for the students taking it now. They use the same writing language as grades 6-9.

  1. Explain every piece of evidence, and answer the other side. Every body paragraph uses Claim-Evidence-Explain, and every argument names the opposing view and responds to it. That is the difference between a 2-2-2 essay and a full-credit one.
  2. Ask why the author did it, in writing, every week. One author's craft question a week on a technique or a literary period, answered with the same frame. This reading domain carries the most weight on the test.
  3. Teach grammar and vocabulary inside reading and drafts. Words come from the texts students are reading, and one convention a week is revised in their own drafts. This was the lowest domain in both reading and writing.

How we'll know: the monthly writing PLC scores Evidence and Elaboration on real student work. Watch the first-semester section closely, since last year's winter group scored well below spring.
